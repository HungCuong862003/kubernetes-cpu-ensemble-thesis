- **DISCOVERY**: all 8 cell-level candidates returned identical pr² = 0.0790. With n=12 and per-dataset-median candidates (3 unique values per feature), the regression is structurally degenerate — any constant-per-dataset candidate gives the same number by construction.

## Substantive findings (the F2 chapter content)

### Finding 1: WPE confirmatory hypothesis nulls across all scopes
| Dataset | Scope | n | pr² | CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cell-level n=12 (locked F2) | pooled | 12 | 0.0790 | percentile [0.000, 0.0790] |
| Alibaba per-series | no_clip | 17,032 | 0.0006 | [0.0000, 0.0023] |
| Alibaba per-series | clip [-1,+1] | 17,032 | 0.0001 | [0.0000, 0.0008] |
| Bitbrains per-series | no_clip | 568 | 0.0544 | [0.0200, 0.1397] |
| Bitbrains per-series | clip [-1,+1] | 568 | 0.1844 | [0.0180, 0.3842] |
| ByteDance per-series | no_clip | 279 | 0.0767 | [0.0003, 0.3136] |
| ByteDance per-series | clip [-1,+1] | 279 | 0.1602 | [0.0049, 0.4358] |

WPE never crosses 0.30. The locked F2 confirmatory test holds.

### Finding 2: EXPLORATORY — three catch22 features cross 0.30 on Bitbrains under clip
| Feature | pr² | CI | Bonferroni status (k=26) |
|---|---|---|---|
| c22_SB_TransitionMatrix_3ac_sumdiagcov | 0.500 | [0.185, 0.697] | survives (CI lower bound above 0.30 threshold under family) |
| c22_SP_Summaries_welch_rect_area_5_1 | 0.374 | [0.030, 0.590] | does NOT survive (CI lower 0.030) |
| c22_DN_OutlierInclude_p_001_mdrmd | 0.309 | [0.030, 0.546] | does NOT survive (CI lower 0.030) |

Two close-misses: c22_FC_LocalSimple_mean3_stderr (pr² 0.297) and dfa_alpha (pr² 0.287).

This finding only appears under clip [−1, +1] on Bitbrains. At no_clip the strongest is SB_TransitionMatrix at 0.196 (still below threshold). The clip-sensitivity is a methodological caveat.

### Finding 3: Per-series scale dramatically reduces ACF + horizon explanatory power on Alibaba
| Scope | r²_reduced(ACF+horizon) |
|---|---|
| Cell-level n=12 (locked F2) | 0.903 |
| Alibaba per-series no_clip | 0.078 |
| Bitbrains per-series no_clip | 0.018 |
| ByteDance per-series no_clip | 0.029 |

ACF + horizon explain less than 8% of per-series delta_pp variance on Alibaba. The cell-level finding does NOT scale down to per-series prediction. This is a chapter-relevant disclosure: the "ACF saturation" claim from the locked F2 holds at cell-level but does not generalise.

### Finding 4: PCA reveals composite ACF axis
Pooled PC1 (34.6%): FC_LocalSimple_mean3_stderr, ACF@1h, DFA-α, Hurst, ACF@24h — all loading together. Suggests the "ACF axis" is not a single metric but a manifold including symbolic forecast-error features.

Three features remain genuinely orthogonal to ACF (low VIF + weak Spearman): CO_trev_1_num (VIF 1.01), DN_HistogramMode_5 (VIF 1.28), LZ76 (VIF 1.87). But all three have low partial-R² on delta_pp.

## Chapter narrative revision (vs Week 0 plan)

The previous "heterogeneous saturation" framing held that no second-axis metric universally substitutes for ACF except LZ76 (consistently orthogonal but uninformative). Week 1 partially supports this framing but adds:

1. **WPE confirmatory still nulls.** Locked F2 holds.
2. **Exploratory finding**: SB_TransitionMatrix carries non-trivial information about delta_pp on Bitbrains (clip [-1,+1] scope). One feature, one dataset, one clip policy. Worth a paragraph in §5 but not a chapter reframe.
3. **Structural finding**: cell-level n=12 is degenerate by construction — any constant-per-dataset candidate returns 0.0790. The locked F2 test was effectively a test of horizon's incremental contribution. This is the most important methodological insight.
4. **Mechanism note**: per-series r²_reduced ranges from 0.018 (Bitbrains) to 0.078 (Alibaba). The cell-level saturation is dominated by between-dataset variance, not within-dataset predictability.
